# Commercial Licensing Guide

This document is a practical summary only. The controlling legal terms are in
[LICENSE](./LICENSE).

## Who Needs A Commercial License

You need a Commercial License from Vitruvian Systems LLC before using the
Software commercially **if**:

- you, together with your Affiliates, have Annual Gross Revenue of
  USD $1,000,000 or more; or
- you were previously below that threshold, used the Software commercially,
  and have now reached or exceeded it.

If you are below the USD $1,000,000 threshold, you may use the Software
commercially under the repository License, including in hosted,
customer-facing, embedded, OEM, or integrator-style offerings, while you
remain below the threshold.

## What The USD $1,000,000 Threshold Means

The threshold is measured using the Annual Gross Revenue of the entire
corporate group, including Affiliates, on a consolidated basis.

In other words, the question is not only what one product earned. The question
is whether the licensee and its Affiliates together meet or exceed the
USD $1,000,000 Annual Gross Revenue threshold defined in the License.

## What To Do If You Cross The Threshold

If you or your corporate group, together with Affiliates, reach or exceed the
USD $1,000,000 Annual Gross Revenue threshold, you must do the following within
30 days:

1. email Vitruvian Systems LLC at emilio@vitruvian.systems;
2. identify your organization and intended continued commercial use; and
3. either obtain a Commercial License or stop the covered commercial use of the
   Software.

You must maintain reasonable records sufficient to show whether you remain
eligible for below-threshold commercial use.

If Vitruvian Systems LLC reasonably asks for confirmation of eligibility, an
authorized representative of your organization must provide a written
certification of compliance.

Upon reasonable written notice and a good-faith basis to believe you may be
using the Software outside the scope of the License, Vitruvian Systems LLC may
request records reasonably necessary to verify compliance with the revenue
threshold, Affiliate aggregation, and other eligibility conditions of the
License.

Continued covered commercial use after loss of below-threshold eligibility and
without obtaining a Commercial License is unauthorized. Vitruvian Systems LLC
reserves the right to seek appropriate license fees, injunctive relief,
enforcement costs, and other remedies available under the License and
applicable law, where permitted.

## FAQ

### We are below USD $1,000,000 and selling a product built with the Software. Is that allowed?
Yes. Below-threshold entities may use the Software commercially under the
repository License while they remain below the threshold.

### We crossed the threshold this quarter. What do we do?
Contact Vitruvian Systems LLC within 30 days to obtain a Commercial License or
stop the covered commercial use.

### Do we need to contact you before crossing the threshold?
Not necessarily, but if you are close to the threshold or expect to cross it
soon, early contact is recommended to avoid interruption.

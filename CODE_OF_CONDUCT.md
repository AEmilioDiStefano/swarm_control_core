# Code Of Conduct

We want `swarm_control_core` to be a respectful, constructive, and technically
serious community.

## Expected Behavior

- be respectful and professional
- assume good intent while staying technically honest
- give actionable, evidence-based feedback
- keep discussions focused on the project and the work

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment, threats, or intimidation
- discrimination or hateful conduct
- bad-faith personal attacks
- doxxing or sharing private information without permission
- deliberate disruption of project spaces

## Reporting

Report Code of Conduct concerns to:

`emilio@vitruvian.systems`

Vitruvian Systems LLC may review reports and take action it considers
appropriate for the health and safety of the project.

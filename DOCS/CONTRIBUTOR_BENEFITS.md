# Contributor Benefits

Vitruvian Systems LLC may provide accepted contributors with non-monetary
recognition and career-support benefits such as:

- public credit in [CONTRIBUTORS.md](./CONTRIBUTORS.md)
- mention in release notes or changelog entries
- permission to cite accepted contributions in resumes, portfolios, job
  applications, and academic applications
- verification of accepted contribution history upon reasonable request
- possible eligibility for elevated repository or community roles after strong
  repeated contributions
- discretionary testimonials, references, or recommendations for standout
  contributors
- possible future consideration for contract or collaborator roles

These benefits are discretionary unless explicitly stated otherwise in writing.

They do not create any right to wages, royalties, equity, profit-sharing,
partnership status, employment status, fiduciary duties, or other financial
compensation.

No financial compensation is owed unless separately agreed in writing.
